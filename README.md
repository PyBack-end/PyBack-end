### FlowOps

Enterprise Operations Platform for Businesses Powered by Void Nexus

FlowOps is a multi-tenant operations platform that manages everything after a business becomes a customer. It acts as the operational core of the Void ecosystem, providing organizations with intelligent workflows, staff management, documents, maintenance, finance, communication, and AI-assisted automation.

### The Ecosystem

```
Void Nexus
Business Discovery
        ↓
Lead Intelligence
        ↓

        ↓
Client Acquisition
        ↓
────────────────────────
        ↓
      FlowOps
Organization Workspace
        ↓
Properties • Staff • Finance
        ↓
Tasks • Documents • AI
```

Stack

`Next.js 14` • `TypeScript` • `Go` • `Python` • `MySQL` • `Prisma` • `Tailwind CSS` • `Redis`

Core Modules

* Tenant & lease management

* Maintenance & inspections

* Financial reporting & payments

* Document intelligence

* Internal messaging

* AI workflow automation

* Event-driven backend services

* API-first modular architecture
